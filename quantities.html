Jet Quantities

Every quantity the formulas may test: 209 for the 8-particle network and 383 for the 64-particle network. Each is computed from the pT, Δη and Δφ (relative to the jet axis) of the particles the network sees, hardest first; empty slots have pT = 0.

Notation: zᵢ = pTᵢ / Σⱼ pTⱼ; ΔRᵢ = √(Δηᵢ² + Δφᵢ²); ΔRᵢⱼ = distance between particles i and j; masses treat particles as massless. λ₁ ≥ λ₂: eigenvalues of Σᵢ zᵢ (Δηᵢ, Δφᵢ)(Δηᵢ, Δφᵢ)ᵀ (spread along and across the jet’s long axis; λ₁ + λ₂ = Σᵢ zᵢΔRᵢ²). N-subjettiness τ_N = Σᵢ zᵢ minₖ ΔR(i, axis k)^β / 0.8^β with N axes from pT-weighted k-means, β = 1 unless noted. Energy correlations: e₂ = Σᵢ<ⱼ zᵢzⱼΔRᵢⱼ, e₃ = Σᵢ<ⱼ<ₖ zᵢzⱼzₖΔRᵢⱼΔRᵢₖΔRⱼₖ, C₂ = e₃/e₂², D₂ = e₃/e₂³; N₂, M₂ use the smallest angles. Soft drop: Cambridge/Aachen clustering of the 20 hardest particles, β = 0, z_cut = 0.1. Mass values offered as candidate thresholds (setups “All observables” and “tuned for agreement”): m_W, m_Z, m_H, m_t and ×½, ×1.5, ×2, for quantities in GeV of mass.

Setups: “no mass observables” removes every quantity in GeV of mass and every ratio built from masses; “no mass observables or exact equivalents” also removes quantities with |correlation| > 0.98 with (m/ΣpT)² on the training jets.
